You know the drill. Scribble frantically during the call. Miss half the conversation because you're focused on capturing action items. Then spend another hour turning your chicken scratch into something coherent.

I tracked it for two weeks. Seven hours of post-meeting admin work. For documents that got opened maybe twice.

The breaking point came during a team meeting where I was so busy taking down feedback that I completely missed identifying a key project risk that surfaced in the conversation. My notes said "looks good" but I was unable to engage in a critical discussion and missed properly documenting it.

>Now I use an AI agent that records, transcribes, and formats everything automatically.

The change is bigger than just time savings. I actually participate in meetings now. I catch the hesitation in someone's voice. I get to identify potential issues and risks and probe further.  I notice when a team member may be checking out rather than effectively engaging in meaningful discussion.

>The agent handles the busy work. I handle the humans.

My meeting notes are more accurate now because the AI catches everything. But more importantly, my meetings are more effective because I'm present for them.

The tool costs me $20 bucks a month. It saves me seven hours a week.

What admin work are you doing that a machine could handle better?
