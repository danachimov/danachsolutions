Welcome. After 35 years leading innovation and project management programs across consumer packaged goods, I have learned that the most useful ideas are rarely the most complicated ones. This blog is where I will share the practical lessons — the ones that actually help teams ship.

## What to expect

Expect short, candid posts on innovation strategy, running an effective PMO, operational excellence, and the responsible adoption of AI. No jargon for its own sake — just what works, what doesn't, and why.

Thanks for reading. If a topic here sparks a question about your own organization, I would welcome the conversation.
